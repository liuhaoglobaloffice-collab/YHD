# Compliance Checklist

## Data Protection

- Verify data classification and retention rules.
- Verify sensitive and high-risk data handling.

## Access Control

- Verify role assignment and permission enforcement.
- Verify tenant isolation and least-privilege records.

## Logging

- Verify that audit logs are produced and retained.
- Verify that operation and workflow activity records are traceable.

## Model Usage Records

- Verify model version and experiment records are stored.
- Verify experimental outcomes are recorded for governance.

## User Data & Ownership

- Verify owner-level data responsibility and deletion workflow.

## Enterprise Responsibility

- Define enterprise data owner, compliance owner, and operations owner.

## Future Standards

The checklist is structured to support future alignment with ISO27001, SOC2, GDPR, and sector-specific regulatory requirements.
