# SLA & Service Management Policy

## Availability Goals

The enterprise AI OS will maintain service availability targets and provide clear operational escalation paths.

- Service availability: 99.9% monthly target
- Fault response target: <15 minutes for P0 and P1, <4 hours for P2
- Recovery target: align with business continuity policy and backup/recovery procedure

## Incident Levels

P0: complete system outage.
P1: core capability failing.
P2: partial workflow or feature degradation.
P3: low-impact or non-critical issue.

## Response & Resolution

- Response time: start triage in the first operational window.
- Resolution time: follow severity target and service window.
- Escalation: incidents with customer impact move to incident commander handling and governance communication.

## Governance Connection

This policy supports the operations manual and enterprise audit/report package.
