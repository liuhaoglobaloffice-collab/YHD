# Security Audit Schedule

## Daily

- Monitor audit logs.
- Watch access anomalies.
- Verify tenants and permissions are active.

## Weekly

- Review abnormal access or denied policy events.
- Confirm workflow and task activity align with the expected tenant scope.

## Monthly

- Audit RBAC assignments.
- Inspect secret handling and rotate where required.
- Verify audit chain and exporter records remain complete.

## Quarterly

- Complete full security audit and compliance review.
- Review compliance readiness for business and regulatory requirements.

## Controls

Check RBAC, tenant isolation, secret management, API permissions, and audit logging.
